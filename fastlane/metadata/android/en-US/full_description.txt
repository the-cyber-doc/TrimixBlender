Trimix Blender works out partial-pressure blends of air, nitrox and trimix: how much oxygen, then helium, then top-up gas to add to a cylinder, and the pressure to read on the gauge at each step.

• Available gases: register your O2, helium and air banks with their pressure. The blend uses the lowest one that works, or the one you choose.
• Target mix: nitrox or trimix, with linked fields: %O2 ↔ MOD ↔ PPO2, and %He ↔ END ↔ gas density at the target depth.
• Gas density warnings against the published limits (5.2 g/l recommended, 6.2 g/l maximum).
• Mix to do: each step drawn as a cylinder filled to scale, and the label to stick on the cylinder.
• Real-gas maths: each gas's compressibility is fitted to NIST reference data. The classic ideal-gas method is a preference.
• Temperature compensation for hot fills.
• When a mix cannot be made, the app says why, and gives the pressure to drain the cylinder down to.
• Metric or imperial units: bar or psi, litres or cu ft, metres or feet, °C or °F.
• 8 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.

Private by design: the app works entirely on your device. It needs no network, asks for no permission, and has no ads and no tracker. Free and open source (GNU GPL v3).

Gas blending is dangerous. Use this app only if you are trained to blend gases, and always analyse the final mix before diving it.
