# Privacy policy

Trimix Blender (the web app, and the Android and iOS apps, `com.dolphinslabs.trimixblender`) collects no data.

- Everything is computed on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere: the Android and iOS apps never use the
  network, and the web app only downloads its own files from the server that hosts it.
- Your preferences (units, language) and your last blend are saved on your device only (the browser's or the app's
  local storage). Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data, deletes them.
- No account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracker, no third-party SDK.
- The apps ask for no permission.

Links in the app (source code, references) open in your browser; the sites they lead to have their own privacy
policies.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/the-cyber-doc/TrimixBlender/issues>.
